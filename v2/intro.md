# Welcome to the Theater of War: Game Introduction

Welcome, Commander. 

If you are accustomed to traditional wargames where victory is determined solely by who rolls the largest stack of plastic miniatures, prepare to adjust your strategy. This game is a modern, high-intensity tactical simulation where **firepower is useless without fuel**, and **supply lines are the literal arteries of victory**. 

Before you dive into the comprehensive rule set in `rules.md`, this introduction will prepare you for the unique strategic pillars, mechanical shifts, and logistical mindsets required to dominate the global map.

---

## The Three Pillars of Victory

To survive and conquer, you must master three core concepts that differentiate this game from standard tabletop wargames:

### 1. Logistics and the Oil Economy
In this game, war is an industrial enterprise. Your units require capital (IPCs) to produce, and your empire requires oil to generate that capital.
*   **The Chain of Production:** Raw oil is rolled for at **Refineries** (which are highly lucrative but vulnerable at sea). This oil does not magically teleport to your treasury; it must flow through physical **Pipelines** to a **Production Complex**.
*   **Supply Line Vulnerabilities:** Pipes and roads can be severed. A single enemy infantry unit occupying a pipeline segment or road can instantly cut off your economic processing or block the deployment of newly produced units. You must defend your corridors of supply just as fiercely as your frontlines.

### 2. The Frontline System (No "Doom Stacks")
Tired of games where a player gathers fifty units into a single territory and rolls over the map in an unstoppable wave?
*   **The 5 vs 5 Limit:** When combat is declared in a contested tile, both the attacker and defender must choose **up to 5 frontline units** to participate in combat for that turn. 
*   **Reserves and Composition:** The remaining units wait in the tile as backup. This mechanic shifts the focus from sheer numbers to tactical composition, unit capabilities as not every unit can engage on any other, and careful reserve management.

### 3. High-Risk, Precision Weaponry
Modern warfare involves devastating weapons with unpredictable consequences.
*   **Collateral Damage:** Launching an **ICBM** can vaporize an entire tile, but a critical failure will trigger devastating splash damage affecting all adjacent tiles—potentially wiping out your own advancing forces.
*   **Arsenal & Equipment:** Torpedoes, long-range missiles, landmines, and tactical drones are transportable assets. Deploying them requires careful planning, escorting them safely to the field, and predicting enemy movement.

## Anatomy of a Turn

Unlike traditional turn-based games where players act entirely in isolation, this game utilizes a hybrid turn structure designed to keep all commanders constantly engaged:

1.  **Phase 1: Global Economy (All Players)**
    *   Commanders simultaneously roll for refinery oil production and make crucial purchasing decisions for units, infrastructure, and supply lines.
2.  **Phase 2: Tactical Maneuvers (In Order of Initiative)**
    *   Commanders roll for initiative to determine movement order. You will navigate the grid, transport equipment, contest territories, and trigger hazardous preemptive defenses (like AAA and submarines).
3.  **Phase 3: Resolution & Harvest (All Players)**
    *   Contested tiles are resolved simultaneously through frontline combat. Survivors gather their processed oil, and treasury balances are updated.

## Tactical Advice for Your First Game

*   **Protect Your Arteries:** Do not leave your pipelines undefended. A cheap landmine or a well-placed infantry division can save millions of IPCs in lost oil production.
*   **Diversify Your Frontline:** Since only 5 units fight at a time, bringing 5 different specialized units (e.g., combining Infantry, AAA, and armor) is often far more effective than bringing 10 of the same unit.
*   **Plan the Route:** Remember that land, air, and naval units are built at **Industrial Complexes** and must travel automatically via roads to their respective military/air/naval bases to deploy. Ensure your transit roads remain clear of enemy forces.
*   **Pro tip: be patient:** Don't rush to try to wipe out your enemies, there will be plenty of time to build defences and the rush might backfire at you.
